TECHNICAL MANUAL: INDUSTRIAL PUMP MODEL X200
Document owner: Engineering Team
Last updated: November 2025

1. OVERVIEW
The X200 is a centrifugal industrial pump rated for continuous operation
at flow rates up to 450 liters per minute. It is designed for water
treatment and light industrial fluid transfer applications.

2. INSTALLATION
The pump must be installed on a vibration-dampening base and requires a
minimum clearance of 60cm on all sides for maintenance access. Inlet and
outlet piping must not exceed a 15-degree bend within 1 meter of the
pump housing.

3. WARRANTY
The X200 carries a 24-month manufacturer warranty from the date of
installation, covering parts and labor for manufacturing defects. The
warranty is extendable to 36 months when the pump is serviced under the
annual maintenance contract.

4. MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
Routine inspection is required every 3 months. Impeller replacement is
recommended every 18-24 months under normal operating conditions, or
every 12 months in high-particulate environments.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING
If the pump loses prime, check the suction line for air leaks first.
Unusual vibration during operation usually indicates impeller imbalance
or a failing bearing, and the unit should be shut down within 24 hours
to prevent seal damage.
