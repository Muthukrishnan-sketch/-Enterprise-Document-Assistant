IT SECURITY POLICY
Document owner: Information Security Team
Last updated: February 2026

1. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
All employee passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include one
uppercase letter, one number, and one special character. Passwords must be
changed every 90 days. Reusing any of the last 5 passwords is not allowed.

2. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
MFA is mandatory for all employees accessing company systems remotely,
including email, VPN, and internal tools. MFA must be set up within 48 hours
of receiving system access.

3. DEVICE POLICY
Only company-issued laptops may be used to access internal systems containing
confidential data. Personal devices may only access email and calendar
through the approved mobile app, which enforces remote wipe capability.

4. DATA CLASSIFICATION
Company data is classified into three levels: Public, Internal, and
Confidential. Confidential data (customer records, financial data, source
code) must never be sent over personal email or uploaded to unapproved
cloud storage services.

5. INCIDENT REPORTING
Any suspected security incident, including phishing emails or lost devices,
must be reported to security@company.internal within 1 hour of discovery.
Failure to report a known incident within 24 hours is treated as a policy
violation.

6. VPN ACCESS
VPN access is required to reach internal systems from outside the office
network. VPN sessions automatically time out after 8 hours of inactivity.
