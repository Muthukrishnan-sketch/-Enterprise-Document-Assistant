EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Document owner: Finance Department
Last updated: March 2026

1. GENERAL RULE
All business expenses must be submitted through the Expense Portal within
30 days of the expense being incurred. Expenses submitted after 60 days
will not be reimbursed without VP approval.

2. TRAVEL EXPENSES
Economy class flights are the default for trips under 6 hours. Business
class is approved automatically for flights over 6 hours. Hotel expenses
are capped at $250 per night in major cities and $150 per night elsewhere.

3. MEALS
Meal reimbursement is capped at $75 per day while traveling for business.
Itemized receipts are required for any single meal expense over $25.
Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstance.

4. CLIENT ENTERTAINMENT
Client entertainment expenses require pre-approval from a manager if the
expected cost exceeds $500. All client entertainment expenses must list
the attendees and business purpose.

5. HOME OFFICE EQUIPMENT
Remote employees may claim up to $500 per year for home office equipment,
including monitors, chairs, and keyboards. This allowance resets every
January 1st and does not carry over.

6. MILEAGE
Personal vehicle use for business purposes is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile.
Commuting between home and the primary office is not reimbursable.
