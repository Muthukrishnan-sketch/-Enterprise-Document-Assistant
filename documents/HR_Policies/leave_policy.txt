HR POLICY: LEAVE AND TIME OFF
Document owner: Human Resources
Last updated: January 2026

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
All full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month. Unused annual leave can
be carried forward into the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days. Any balance
above 10 days is forfeited on December 31st unless approved by a manager in
writing.

2. SICK LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical
certificate is required for sick leave lasting more than 2 consecutive days.
Sick leave does not carry forward to the next year.

3. PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave
requests must be submitted at least 30 days before the expected start date.

4. HOW TO APPLY FOR LEAVE
All leave requests must be submitted through the internal HR portal at least
5 working days in advance, except in emergencies. Leave longer than 10
consecutive days requires additional approval from a department head.

5. UNPAID LEAVE
Employees with at least 1 year of tenure may request unpaid leave of up to
90 days for personal reasons, subject to manager and HR approval.
