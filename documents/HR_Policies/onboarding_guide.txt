NEW EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING GUIDE
Document owner: People Operations
Last updated: April 2026

1. BEFORE DAY ONE
IT will ship your laptop 3-5 business days before your start date. You will
receive a welcome email with your company email address and instructions to
set up multi-factor authentication (see IT Security Policy).

2. FIRST WEEK
Your manager will schedule a 1:1 within your first 2 days. All new
employees must complete mandatory compliance training (data privacy,
security awareness, code of conduct) within the first 7 days.

3. BENEFITS ENROLLMENT
Health insurance, retirement plan, and other benefits must be enrolled
within 30 days of your start date. Missing this window means waiting until
the next open enrollment period, unless you have a qualifying life event.

4. PROBATION PERIOD
All new hires have a 90-day probation period, during which either party may
end employment with 1 week's notice. Performance check-ins happen at the
30-day and 60-day marks.

5. ACCESS AND TOOLS
Access to internal tools (Slack, Jira, the HR portal) is provisioned
automatically on day one. Access to systems containing confidential data
requires manager approval and is provisioned within 48 hours of request.
