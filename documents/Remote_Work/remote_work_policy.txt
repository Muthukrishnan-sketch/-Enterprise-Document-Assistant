REMOTE WORK AND HOME OFFICE POLICY
Document owner: Human Resources
Last updated: May 2026

1. ELIGIBILITY
Employees who have completed their 90-day probation period are eligible to
work remotely, subject to manager approval and role suitability. Employees
in fully remote roles, as defined in their offer letter, are not subject to
the day limits described in Section 2.

2. REMOTE WORK DAYS
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Team leads may
set stricter limits based on coverage needs, but may not exceed the 3-day
company maximum without approval from a department head. In-office days
must include at least one team-wide collaboration day, as scheduled by the
employee's manager.

3. HOME OFFICE EQUIPMENT REIMBURSEMENT
Employees approved for regular remote work are eligible for a one-time home
office equipment reimbursement of up to $500 (or the local currency
equivalent) within their first 90 days of remote eligibility. Covered items
include: desk, ergonomic chair, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset.
Reimbursement requests must be submitted with itemized receipts through the
Expenses portal within 60 days of purchase. Items not listed above require
pre-approval from a manager before purchase to qualify for reimbursement.

In addition, remote employees receive a recurring monthly allowance of $40
toward home internet and electricity costs, paid automatically with payroll
and requiring no separate claim.

4. SECURITY REQUIREMENTS FOR REMOTE WORK
All remote work must be performed using company-issued devices connected
through the corporate VPN, per the IT Security Policy. Use of public Wi-Fi
without an active VPN connection is prohibited. Printing confidential
documents at a home location is not permitted. Devices must lock
automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

5. CORE HOURS AND AVAILABILITY
Remote employees are expected to be reachable on company messaging tools
during core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, regardless of their
broader working schedule. Extended unavailability during core hours must be
communicated to the employee's manager in advance.

6. APPROVAL PROCESS
Remote work arrangements are requested through the internal HR portal,
the same system used for leave requests. Initial approval is granted by
the employee's direct manager; recurring remote arrangements longer than
3 months require an annual review and re-approval.
