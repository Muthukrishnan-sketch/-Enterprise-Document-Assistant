REMOTE WORK POLICY
Document owner: People Operations
Last updated: January 2026

1. ELIGIBILITY
Employees are eligible for remote work after completing 90 days of
employment, subject to manager approval and role requirements. Roles that
require on-site presence (lab work, facilities, front-desk) are not
eligible for remote work.

2. HYBRID SCHEDULE
Hybrid employees are required to be in the office at least 2 days per week,
with Tuesday designated as a mandatory in-office day company-wide.

3. FULLY REMOTE ARRANGEMENTS
Fully remote arrangements require director-level approval and are reviewed
annually. Fully remote employees must be available during core hours of
10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their team's primary timezone.

4. INTERNATIONAL REMOTE WORK
Working remotely from a country other than your employment country is
allowed for a maximum of 20 working days per year and requires approval
from HR and Legal at least 2 weeks in advance, due to tax and visa
implications.

5. EQUIPMENT AND CONNECTIVITY
The company provides a laptop and a one-time $500 home office stipend (see
Expense Policy). Employees are responsible for maintaining a stable internet
connection of at least 25 Mbps for video calls.
